Cheltenham, 1882 — a nineteen-year-old medical student watched a tall woman in black, her face hidden in a handkerchief held in her right hand, come down the stairs of her family's house. She did not run. She started taking notes: dates, times, who was present, what each person saw. She spoke to the figure and got "a slight gasp" and nothing more. She tried to touch it and her hand met nothing. She cornered it in a room with one door and it was simply gone. So she strung threads across the staircase in the dark — and the figure came down through them without displacing one. Seventeen people saw the woman in black between 1882 and 1886; most took her for a living visitor. Her record was published by the Society for Psychical Research in 1892 and called one of the best authenticated hauntings ever documented. Rosina Despard went on to become the twenty-third woman in England to take a medical degree, and a prison physician. Dramatised from her own published account. 🎙️ How would you have tested it?
