# TRANSMISSION — "PASSENGER" (Part 1) — draft v1

The user's combination of ideas 10 (Seatbelt) and 13 (Blind Spot), with Out of Hours and fog: a young London
driver out of hours in thick fog has to stop in a lay-by on the A66 over Stainmore and sleep in his bunk.
His side sensor goes off with the key out of the ignition, faster and faster up the passenger side, then the
passenger seatbelt chime. Both sounds are planted in the first 30 seconds so the ending needs no explaining.
Tony, his night planner, is on the hands-free the whole way. Ends on the click of the belt going in
(my suggestion; the user's version ends on the chime: a one-second cut).

[Cab, driving slowly in fog. Engine, wipers. Ding... ding... ding — the passenger seatbelt chime.]
JAMIE: Oh, shut up.
TONY (hands-free): What's that?
JAMIE: Passenger seatbelt. It thinks me bag's a person.
[The bag thrown into the bunk. The chime stops.]
TONY: Where are you?
JAMIE: Top of Stainmore. Fog's like soup. And I'm out of hours in ten minutes.
TONY: So where you stopping?
JAMIE: Bowes Moor lay-by.
TONY: ...Nobody parks there.
JAMIE: Why?
TONY: Ah, ignore me. Drivers' talk.
[Indicator. Slowing onto gravel. The side sensor beeps, faster, as he pulls in tight to the barrier.]
JAMIE: Yeah, alright, I can see it!
[Air brakes.]
TONY: What's that one?
JAMIE: Side sensor. Beeps if anything's down the passenger side.
[Engine off.] JAMIE: Right. Engine off. [Keys.] Key out. [Locks.] Doors locked. [Curtains.] Curtains shut.
TONY: You alright?
JAMIE: Don't like it up here, Tone. It's too quiet.
TONY: Want me to sing you to sleep?
JAMIE: Do one.
TONY: [laughs] Big baby.
[He climbs into the bunk. The night heater. The engine ticking as it cools. Quiet.]
[BEEP.]
JAMIE (quiet): That's the side sensor.
TONY: You said the key's out.
JAMIE: It is. It's up here with me. [keys]
[Beep... beep... beep.. beep. beep-beep-beep — faster and faster]
JAMIE (whisper): Tone. It's coming up the passenger side.
[A continuous tone. It stops dead.]
TONY: Jamie?
JAMIE: It's stopped.
TONY: Doors?
JAMIE: Locked.
[Silence.]
[Ding. Ding. Ding.]
TONY: Is that your bag?
JAMIE: It's in here with me.
[Ding. Ding. — CLICK.]
CUT. — PART 1 / FOLLOW FOR PART 2
