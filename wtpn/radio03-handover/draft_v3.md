Thirty wagons, all back in. Rain all night.

0:00  The office. Rain on the window. Radio hiss. Far off across the yard: a wagon's air brakes, then
      the barrier: motor whine, warning beeps, the arm coming down.          [PLANT 1: the barrier]
ASH: That's the last one in. Barrier's down.
MICK: Champion. (keys onto a hook on the board: a soft jingle)                [PLANT 2: the keys]
MICK: That's thirty home. Keys are on the board.
ASH: What's Steve left you?
MICK: (paper) Usual. OEU has an AdBlue fault, for a change. Wakefield's moved to half five.
MICK: "Don't let anyone in after three." Underlined. "Not even me." Underlined twice.
ASH: He's on a wind-up.
MICK: Daft sod's left his phone an' all. (it buzzes on the desk) ... Fourteen missed calls. No number.
ASH: Bookies.

0:32  Rain.
ASH: Three o'clock. Gate's locked, barrier's down, it's siling it down and I'm frozen. All quiet.
MICK: Nobody in after three, Ash.
ASH: Not even Steve.

0:44  A long gap. Rain. Hiss.
ASH: Mick. You'll not believe this. Steve's at the gate.
      (buried under the line, barely there: the barrier motor. Nobody mentions it.)   [the re-listen]
MICK: You what?
ASH: On foot. Says he's left his phone.
MICK: He has. It's here.
ASH: So I'll let him in, then.
MICK: What's the note say?
ASH: Oh, give over. He's wet through.
MICK: Where's his car?
ASH: ... He's walked.
MICK: He lives out Holmfirth. That's twenty mile.

1:04
ASH: He'll not look up at the camera. He keeps looking up the lane.
STEVE: (faint, through the glass) Is he coming down?
ASH: He's asking for you. Not the phone. You.
STEVE: (faint) Please, Ash.
MICK: Steve's never said please in his life.

1:16  Mick's mobile on speaker on the desk. Ringing. Ringing.
KAREN: (woken) Hello?
MICK: Karen, it's Mick at the yard. Is Steve there?
KAREN: No, love. Someone rang the house about half two. He went white. Went straight out. Wouldn't say who.
KAREN: He said if you rang, I've to tell you to read his note again.
MICK: (quietly, to himself, not on the radio) Not even me.
KAREN: Mick? Is he with you? Is he all right?
MICK: I'll ring you back, love. (the call ends)

1:36
ASH: Mick. There's summat up the lane.
MICK: What?
ASH: An engine. Just ticking over. No lights. I never heard it come.
STEVE: (faint) Please.
ASH: I'm letting him in.
MICK: Don't you dare.
ASH: He's stood in the rain, Mick. It's Steve!
MICK: Don't open it. I'm coming down.

1:50  The handset pulled off its charger. The chair. The office door, rain louder, the door shuts.
      Then the empty office: rain on the window, the hiss, nothing else. Eight seconds.

2:00  Squelch. Mick outdoors, out of breath, rain on the handset.
MICK: Ash. Where is he?
ASH: (beside him, off the mic) Where's who?
MICK: Steve.
ASH: Steve's not been here, mate. Radio's been quiet all night.
MICK: Why's the barrier up?
ASH: It's not, it's— (a beat) It says it's been up since twenty past three.
      (rain; Mick's breathing; he keeps the button down)
MICK: Ash... who's that in my office?

2:12  The transmission drops. The office. Rain on the window. The hiss.
      Then, close to us, soft: keys lifted off the board.
Cut to black.
