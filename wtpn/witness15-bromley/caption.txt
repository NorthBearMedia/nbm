Grove Park allotments, Bromley, 1973 — three pensioners ran a garden-supplies hut called the Kentish Garden Guild. Seed potatoes, fertiliser, somewhere to go of a morning. On the 26th of April, powder shot up and hit the ceiling and a small jug flew across the room; shut inside a covered box, the jug was found back on the floor. Then objects began circling the hut "like skittles", fertiliser came out of the bins on its own, and a seven-pound weight orbited one man's head. After he tried to face it down, he came out cut and bruised — and the next morning the shed looked as if a bomb had hit it, with the sign of the cross scratched and painted on the walls, the chairs, the bins. The Society for Psychical Research sent two investigators, who watched objects hang in the air, bolts vanish, the shirt ripped off his back with a saw pushed down it, and a bulb forced into his mouth. They found no deception. It ran for nearly two years, in a shed on an allotment, and one of the investigators published the case under his name. Dramatised from the SPR case record. 🎙️ Why him?
