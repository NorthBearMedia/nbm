# WITNESS 14 — The Cage, St Osyth, Essex, 2004-2008. Witness: Vanessa Mitchell.

Vanessa Mitchell is a LIVING named person who has published her own account:
co-author of "Spirits of the Cage: True Accounts of Living in a Haunted Medieval
Prison" (with Richard Estep, Llewellyn, 2017), plus press interviews (Clacton and
Frinton Gazette) and TV (ITV This Morning; Channel 4 "True Horror: A Witches'
Prison", 2018). USE ONLY HER PUBLISHED CLAIMS — no embellishment whatsoever.

Cinematic Cage episode ran 9 Aug (EP29); witness retell is 31 days later = gap met.

## Facts used
- The Cage, St Osyth, Essex: a small cottage that incorporates the village lock-up
  used in the 16th century to hold people accused of witchcraft — including during
  the St Osyth witch trials. (Historic fact; real people were imprisoned and some
  executed. Treat with respect; do NOT identify any apparition with a named
  historical woman.)
- Vanessa Mitchell bought it and moved in in 2004 with her baby son.
- HER CLAIMS (published): apparitions walking through her room from the first day;
  ghostly growls; being SLAPPED and PUSHED by invisible hands; being pushed over
  while pregnant; mysterious blood spatters appearing; activity centred on the
  house while her young son was there.
- She left in 2008 — about three years of living there — "fearing for her young
  son's safety"; the Gazette reported she "fled the house after she was physically
  attacked".
- She kept the property and it became a paranormal-investigation venue; she later
  put it up for sale (widely covered 2019; reported difficulty selling).
- She co-wrote the book with investigator Richard Estep in 2017 and has repeated
  the account on national television.

## Dramatised (texture only)
- Ordinary-life framing (a village cottage, a young mum, the school run register);

  fact that she bought it as a home; interviewer cards.
- Her stated reason for leaving is documented; the interiority around it is
