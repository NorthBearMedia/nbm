St Osyth, Essex, 2004 — a woman bought a small cottage in the village she grew up in. Part of the building was the old lock-up, where people accused of witchcraft were held four hundred years ago. She bought it as a home, and moved in with her baby son. On the first day, she says, a figure walked through her room. Then came the growling in empty rooms, the spots of blood appearing on a floor with nobody bleeding, and the thing she says people go quiet about: being slapped, shoved and pushed by hands that were not there — once while she was pregnant. She stayed three years, then looked at her son asleep in that house and left. She has since put her name to a book about it with a paranormal investigator, and repeated the account on national television. She still owns the building.
