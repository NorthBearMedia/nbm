Battersea, South London, 1956 — she was fifteen. Her father, a train driver, stood in her bedroom and watched his daughter lift off her bed. He told the papers exactly what he saw — rigid, about six inches above the mattress — and never took a word of it back. Neither has she: she told the BBC the same story in 2021, in her eighties.
