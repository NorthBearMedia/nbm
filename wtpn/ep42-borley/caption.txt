Borley Rectory, Essex — the house England still calls the most haunted it ever had. Pencil writing appeared low on the bedroom walls, begging Marianne Foyster for help, by name — for prayers, for a mass. She scrubbed it off. It came back. They photographed it. In 1937 a ghost hunter took the lease and moved in 48 volunteer observers, who logged objects moving, voices, and a nun walking the garden path. Then the house burned in the night and the ruin was pulled down to the last brick. It did not matter. The nun is still seen — in the churchyard, in the fields where the garden was. And nobody ever found out who spent five years writing for help in pencil. Nobody ever helped her. 🕯️ Who do you think was writing?
