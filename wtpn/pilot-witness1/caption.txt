Enfield, North London, 1977 — a police officer was called to a council house at 1 a.m. and watched an armchair slide across the floor with nobody near it. She got on her knees and searched it for wires. There was nothing. So she wrote it up, and she signed her name to it — and in fifty years she has never taken it back. This is her account, in interview, dramatised from her signed statement and broadcast interviews: the silent children on the stairs, the chair that stopped "like it had done what it came to do", and the part she says she still thinks about. 🎙️ Would you have signed your name to it?
