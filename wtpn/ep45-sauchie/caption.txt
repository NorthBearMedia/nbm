Sauchie, Clackmannanshire, Scotland — in 1960 a teacher watched an empty desk lid rise and fall in front of a full classroom, and she never changed her story. It followed an 11-year-old called Virginia Campbell, just arrived from Donegal: knocking that moved with her from room to room, a sideboard that walked out from the wall, the chest beside her bed rocking while a minister watched. Two doctors saw it. The BBC broadcast the tape of the knocking. At school her teacher, Miss Stewart, watched Virginia's desk lift off the floor. Ministers prayed in the house and that winter it faded — but two doctors, a minister, a teacher and twenty children never took a word of it back, and the case has never been explained. The recording still exists. The street is still there. Nobody knows what followed Virginia over from Ireland — or where it went next. 🎙️ What do you think followed her?
