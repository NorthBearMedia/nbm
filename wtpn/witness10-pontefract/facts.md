# WITNESS 10 — Pontefract / 30 East Drive, 1966-69. Witness: Diane Pritchard.

The Pritchard family's accounts were documented at length by investigator Colin
Wilson (Poltergeist!: A Study in Destructive Haunting, 1981, from family
interviews) and retold consistently by the family for decades. The episode
dramatises Diane's experience as the phenomenon's focus.

## Sources
- Rue Morgue "Paranormal Pontefract" (timeline, dust/puddles, clock, Diane
  pinned/mattress, the drag with throat marks, garlic ending)
- Villains Wiki / Paranormal Encounters / anomalien (Wilson quotes: dragged up
  the stairs by "an invisible hand that left lacerations on her neck")
- 30eastdrive.com + Haunted Happenings (the house operates ghost-hunt stays)
- Wilson, Poltergeist! (1981) — Google Books listing

## Facts used
- Family: Joe, Jean, Philip (15) and Diane (12) Pritchard, moved to 30 East
  Drive, Pontefract, Aug 1966.
- PHASE 1 (1966): fine dust falling from mid-air; pools of water on the floor
  with no source found; then ~two quiet years.
- PHASE 2 (late 1968 - mid 1969): returned stronger. Large grandmother clock
  thrown down the stairs; objects thrown; furniture moving.
- DIANE (the focus): repeatedly thrown from her bed; woke on the floor with
  the mattress on top of her; pinned by furniture heavy enough to crush her;
  DRAGGED UP THE STAIRS by an unseen force — lacerations / clearly visible
  finger marks left on her throat (Wilson).
- THE BLACK MONK: a tall figure in a black monk's habit, cowl over the head,
  reportedly witnessed in the house (who saw it varies by retelling — kept
  unattributed in the script).
- Press dubbed it "Mr Nobody" (1968); the family called it "Fred".
- 1969: the family was advised to hang garlic around the house; shortly after,
  the haunting ceased.
- The house still stands and hosts paying overnight ghost hunts.
- The family's account remained consistent; called one of the most violent
  poltergeist cases in Europe.

## Dramatised (texture only)
- Diane's interiority ("You'd be amazed what you can forget"; "It felt better,
  having it named. It shouldn't have."); "my family pulled me free" kept
  non-specific (retellings vary on who); interviewer cards.

## DO NOT USE
- The murdered-nun/Cluniac monk backstory as fact (legend attached later).
- Naming WHO saw the monk (sources vary).
- Any claim doctors examined/photographed the marks (not verified).
- Aunt Maude/green foam/eggs (not verified in this pass — omit).
