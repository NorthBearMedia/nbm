# TRANSMISSION 04 — "MONITOR" (Part 1) — draft v1

Terraced house, East London. 3 a.m. Danny's first night alone with the baby; Kel is on a night shift at the
hospital, on speakerphone on the kitchen table, next to the baby monitor (our radio). Built out from the acting
test the user picked (A: v3 dialogue). Planted early: Kel's shush, "three long and a short one".

[MONITOR: hiss. A baby crying, worn out, hiccupping.]
KEL (phone, on speaker, a ward behind her): Did you do the shush? Three long and a short one.
DANNY: I did, like... two long ones.
KEL: [laughs] Dan.
[On the monitor, the crying stops. Dead. Mid-breath.]
DANNY: ...She's stopped.
KEL: See? Told you she'd settle.
DANNY: Just like that?
KEL: Just like that. Go put the kettle on, you've earned it.
[Hiss. Then on the monitor: a slow creak. And again. A rhythm.]
DANNY: What's that?
KEL: What's what?
DANNY: That creaking. On the monitor.
KEL: That's the chair. The feeding chair does that when you rock it.
DANNY: Kel... nobody's in the chair.
[The creaking goes on, slow, patient. Under it, faint: shushing.]
DANNY (quiet): Kel. Did you come home?
KEL: What? No, I'm at work. Why?
DANNY: Someone's in with her.
KEL: What?
DANNY: Listen.
[He turns the monitor up. The chair. The shushing, clear: three long, and a short one. Three long... and a short one.]
KEL (barely): That's how I do it.
[The shushing stops dead. So does the chair.]
KEL: Danny. Get up there.
[Monitor: the chair creaks as someone gets up out of it. Footsteps cross the nursery. The door.]
[Silence on the monitor.]
[In the kitchen, close: a stair creaks.]
DANNY (whisper): Kel. Someone's on the stairs.
[Monitor: the baby starts to cry again. On her own.]
KEL (whisper): Danny... she's on her own.
[The next stair down. Closer.]

QC notes
1. Hook inside ~6 s: the crying stops dead (not after banter).
2. Clear with no picture: crying = baby; "on the monitor" said aloud; ward behind Kel + "I'm at work";
   the chair is named ("the feeding chair"); the shush rhythm is planted and countable; upstairs/kitchen/stairs.
3. Likeable: Danny can't do the shush right and knows it; Kel teases him, warm. Nobody miserable.
4. Never shown: we never learn what it is. Everything is a sound on a cheap speaker or a stair in the dark.
5. Not the old endings: no copied voice (Crest), no knock or lights (Corner). The end is a choice: the baby is
   crying upstairs and whatever came out of her room is on the stairs between them.
