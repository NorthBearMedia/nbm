# WITNESS 7 — South Shields poltergeist, 2006. Witness: Darren W. Ritson (investigator).

Ritson is a real, named psychical researcher who co-investigated the case with
Michael J. Hallowell and co-wrote *The South Shields Poltergeist: One Family's
Fight Against an Invisible Intruder* (The History Press, 2008). The episode
dramatises HIS published first-person account and retrospective interviews.

## Sources
- Spooky Isles: "The South Shields Poltergeist, 15 Years On" — Ritson interview
  with extensive verbatim first-person quotes (all quotes below from here)
- Amazon/AbeBooks listings of the book (publication facts; Guy Lyon Playfair
  credited as foreword contributor)
- Europaranormal / GSNSP case summaries (phenomena list, 2005-2006 timeline,
  500+ photographs, video and audio archive)

## Facts used
- South Shields, Tyneside; case ran late 2005 through most of 2006. A young
  couple and their small boy — named "Marc", "Marianne" and "Robert" in the
  published record, all PSEUDONYMS; the published address is also fictionalised.
- Ritson & Hallowell investigated for months; compiled 500+ digital photographs,
  dozens of hours of video, extensive audio logs.
- WITNESSED BY RITSON (his words): a yellow plastic nut from a toy workbench
  "flew across the room with such speed and force; it then bounced off a wooden
  cupboard door before hitting Marianne"; "a rubber ball appeared from nowhere
  on Robert's bed – the ball was actually hot to touch"; "watching a child's toy
  appear in the top corner of the room – that had seemingly materialised from
  out of thin air – then drop suddenly to the floor below".
- KNIVES: "I actually found a large silver kitchen knife lying in situ on
  Robert's bedroom floor, and it was certainly not there previously"; he watched
  "a carving knife as it hurled itself across the kitchen, bounced off the TV
  that was perched high on a shelf before clattering to the floor".
- MARC'S INJURIES: his upper torso/back slashed with lacerations/scratches
  without human intervention (documented in the case file and book).
- DOODLE BOARD: written messages on the child's doodle board: "go now",
  "stop it now".
- TEXT MESSAGES to Marianne's phone while the phones they seemingly came from
  were DISABLED: "Going to die today, going to get you"; "I can get you when
  you awake and I'll come for you when you're asleep". (Other messages contain
  a slur-adjacent insult — NOT used on screen; see DO NOT USE.)
- FRAUD CONTROL (Ritson verbatim): "Our eyes were transfixed on Marc and
  Marianne, observing their every move… we can say with one hundred percent
  certainty that it was not them responsible for these actions."
- Guy Lyon Playfair — the investigator who documented Enfield 1977 — is
  credited on the book (foreword). [Internal callback: our Enfield witness ep.]
- NEVER RECANTED (verbatim): "There is certainly more to this world than we can
  know at this present moment in time, and that I can say with total conviction
  after investigating this case with Michael J. Hallowell."
