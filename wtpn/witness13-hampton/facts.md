# WITNESS 13 — Hampton Court Palace CCTV, 2003. Witness: James Faukes, security guard.

A named professional witness with VERBATIM quotes given to the press/BBC at the
time. The channel ran a cinematic Hampton Court episode 16 Aug (EP37); this
witness retell is 23 days later (8 Sep) — gap met.

## Sources
- NBC News (AP/Reuters wire), 19 Dec 2003, "Ghostly image at Britain's Hampton
  Court" — all verbatim quotes below
- Wikipedia "Hampton Court ghost" (Oct 2003, robe-like cloth)
- Historic Royal Palaces' own "Historic hauntings at Hampton Court Palace"
- Hampton Court Palace's own social post "Skeletor - the CCTV ghost": "On three
  consecutive nights in the winter of 2003, Hampton Court Palace security staff
  were alerted to the opening of a fire door"

## Facts used (verbatim where quoted)
- October 2003, Hampton Court Palace. A fire door kept being found open;
  security staff were alerted to it on three consecutive occasions and went to
  the CCTV to find out who was doing it.
- The footage showed the doors opening, and a figure in "a robelike garment"
  with an unnaturally pale face stepping from a doorway, one arm reaching for
  the door handle, and closing them.
- JAMES FAUKES, security guard (verbatim, to press/BBC):
  "It was incredibly spooky because the face just didn't look human."
  "My first reaction was that someone was having a laugh, so I asked my
  colleagues to take a look. We spoke to our costumed guides, but they don't
  own a costume like that."
- VIKKI WOOD, palace spokeswoman (verbatim): "We're baffled too — it's not a
  joke, we haven't manufactured it." / "We genuinely don't know who it is or
  what it is."
- No one was ever identified. The costumed Tudor guides confirmed they did not
  own such a costume. The image was released in December 2003 and went worldwide.
- The palace itself still discusses the footage publicly (its own channels
  nickname the figure "Skeletor").
