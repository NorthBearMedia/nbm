Why we did it

We are raising money for allergy awareness. It is a cause close to the office, and one that affects a lot more families than most people realise.

How it worked

Three exercise bikes, in the office, from eight in the morning until seven at night, Monday to Friday. Everyone took half hour slots around their actual work, and every half hour added about six miles to the total.

Added together over the week, that is the distance from Land's End to John o'Groats. The tracker above shows how we got on.

If you would like to sponsor us, every penny goes to the charity.
